# Blog 1 
What is Data Science to Me?

When I think about data science, I usually think about numbers, charts, graphs, and computers. However, I am starting to understand that data science can also be used to learn more about people and society. I think learning data science in social and interdisciplinary settings is important because data is connected to real people and real situations. It is not just about looking at numbers. We also have to understand where the data comes from and what it can tell us about the world around us.

One reason we model social topics and people is because data can help us see patterns that we might not notice on our own. For example, data can be used to study things like education, social media, jobs, or how people communicate. Looking at these patterns can help us understand different problems and possibly find ways to solve them. At the same time, I think it is important to remember that data cannot explain everything about people. Everyone is different, so a model might not always show the full picture.

There are also responsibilities that come with using data about people. One of the biggest responsibilities is protecting people's privacy. We should be careful about collecting and sharing personal information. It is also important to think about bias in data. If the data is incomplete or unfair, the results may also be unfair or incorrect. People who work with data should think about how their work could affect others instead of only focusing on getting results.

Through this course, I hope to better understand how data science connects with other subjects. I want to learn how data can be used to understand real-world problems and how to use it responsibly. I also hope to become more comfortable reading and understanding data. Overall, I want to learn how data science can help us understand the world while also remembering that there are real people behind the data.
